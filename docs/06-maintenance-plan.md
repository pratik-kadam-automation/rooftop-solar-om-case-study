# Maintenance plan

What changed: cleaning went from every one or two months, with no plan, to a planned cycle of about twice a month. I
added cable and connector checks, insulation resistance tests before the monsoon, a daily late-string review and inverter
cooling.

The calendar is in [`templates/pm_calendar.csv`](../templates/pm_calendar.csv). It is a starting point, so adjust it to
your site, the maker's instructions and your standards.

## Safety

PV DC circuits can stay live in daylight. Use isolation, lockout and tagout, arc-flash precautions, work-at-height
controls and roof load limits. Only qualified people should test insulation resistance, open enclosures or inspect live
equipment.

## Thermography: what I found

I tested it. It was done when the plant was installed, then not again for years. Now I do it about once a year, or when
there is a reason. On this plant it hasn't added much so far. The faults that cost generation were found faster by the
daily string data, the insulation resistance tests and plain inspection.

I still keep it as a periodic check, because it is a quick way to look for hot connectors and hot cells without touching
anything. If you do it, it only means something when you record the conditions: irradiance, ambient temperature, load or
current, wind and the time. Compare similar parts under similar conditions. Keep to safe approach distances, and don't
open a live enclosure just to get a picture.

## Cleaning

Clean on condition, not on habit. Check the energy response before and after. If cleaning more often recovers nothing,
the loss is somewhere else.
